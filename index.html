em·ji·ve

Designing and building the website of a jewellery brand I co-founded, then iterating on it with early test users.

Dates
Since July 2026
Tools
HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Affinity, Blender
Website
emjive.eu

Context

em·ji·ve is a creative umbrella for every project my brother and I co-lead. Its first study is a collection of a dozen rings. We share one observation: the world is bloated with capitalist solicitations. Our studies are an effort to produce innovative design, stripped of any bloat.

Problem

The website has to be both a gallery showcasing our studies and an e-commerce shop. It must guide each type of visitor to what they are looking for, without compromising the brand's identity.

Result

A hybrid website that borrows from laboratories, clinical settings and small gallery exhibitions to form a minimalist home for the brand, with shortcuts for visitors who came to shop and have no interest in the brand itself.

Guisarde add-on

A Google Drive add-on for a creative agency that turns one form into a full project folder tree.

Dates
April – June 2026
Tools
Google Apps Script, Google Drive
Users
The agency's two CEOs

Context

The team relied on Google Drive to store live project folders, from sales negotiations through production, and built and moved every project folder by hand.

Problem

Doing everything by hand caused two issues. First, the team lost time repeating the same tasks every time a project folder was created or moved. Second, inconsistencies crept into the files: naming conventions not followed, missing sub-folders, and so on.

Result

An agency-wide Google Drive add-on that builds and moves project folders according to the agency's conventions. It cuts the time spent on project folder admin by about 95%, from roughly 30 hours a year to 1.5, and every folder it creates follows the same naming and structure.

ACE

A Blender add-on that converts live audio into data, with a node-based alternative to Blender's add-on UI panels.

Dates
September – December 2025
Tools
Python, Blender API

Context

I make 3D animations and produce music as hobbies, and I wanted to merge the two by making audio-reactive animations for my music.

Problem

Blender, which I use for my 3D animations, is limited for audio-reactive work. The only existing workflow is a tool that bakes an audio file into keyframes. That means I can't test the audio-reactive behaviour in real time, and I'm very limited in how I can split the audio information to drive different parts of the animation.

Result

An audio analysis engine with a set of operators inspired by TouchDesigner, the industry leader, in a node-based interface that integrates cleanly with Blender's Geometry Nodes editor for real-time 3D geometry manipulation.
